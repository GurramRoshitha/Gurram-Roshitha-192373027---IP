Booking Workflow

1. Login or register

Sign in using valid credentials. The passenger dashboard collects journey, passenger, origin, destination and class details.

2. Select trains and seats

The system shows the available train list, seat availability and fare details for the chosen route and date.

3. Pay and confirm

Choose online or offline payment. After successful payment, the system generates the ticket and assigns a unique PNR number.

4. Track and manage

Passengers can check PNR status, view booking history, print tickets, or request cancellations with refund processing.
